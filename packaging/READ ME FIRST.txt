FAIRGAME - QUICK START
======================

FairGame watches Amazon for an item and buys it automatically when it comes
in stock at a price you choose.

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
1. Install Google Chrome if you don't have it: https://www.google.com/chrome
2. On Amazon's website, make sure your default shipping address and payment
   method are set. FairGame uses your defaults and cannot choose them for you.
3. Empty your Amazon cart. FairGame won't start if there's anything in it.

OPENING FAIRGAME
----------------
Windows:
  Open the FairGame folder and double-click FairGame.exe.
  (Leave the other files where they are; FairGame needs them.)
  If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
  "Run anyway". This happens because the app isn't from a registered company.

Mac (Apple Silicon only, M1 or newer):
  Drag FairGame into your Applications folder if you like, then double-click
  it. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app. Click "Done", open
  System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next
  to FairGame, then open it again.

Linux:
  Open the FairGame folder and run FairGame.

USING FAIRGAME
--------------
1. Items tab: paste the Amazon link of the product you want (copy it from
   your browser's address bar) and type the lowest and highest price you're
   willing to pay. Click "Add another item" to buy more than one thing.
2. Account tab: enter your Amazon email and password, and make up a
   FairGame password. The FairGame password locks your Amazon login on this
   computer; you'll type it every time you start.
3. Leave "Test mode" switched ON the first time and click Start.

A Chrome window opens and FairGame works in it. Leave that window alone.
Watch the Activity tab and the status at the bottom. In test mode it goes
all the way to the checkout page but does NOT buy anything. When it says
"Test complete", it's working.

To buy for real, switch Test mode off and click Start. Click Stop at any
time.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Amazon may restrict accounts that use bots. Don't run FairGame on more
  than one computer at a time with the same Amazon account, and don't set
  the time between checks too low (Options tab).
- If Chrome offers to save your password, click "Never".
- If Amazon asks for a code or a puzzle, answer it in the Chrome window.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- Forgot your FairGame password: on the Account tab click "Use a different
  Amazon account" and sign in again.
- Something went wrong: the Activity tab has buttons to open screenshots of
  each step and the log files.
- Options > "Reset Chrome profile" fixes most Chrome start-up problems.
