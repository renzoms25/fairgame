FAIRGAME - QUICK START
======================

FairGame watches Amazon for an item and buys it automatically when it comes
in stock at a price you choose.

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
1. Install Google Chrome if you don't have it: https://www.google.com/chrome
2. On Amazon's website, make sure your default shipping address and payment
   method are set. FairGame uses your defaults and cannot choose them for you.
3. Empty your Amazon cart. FairGame won't start if there's anything in it.

STARTING FAIRGAME
-----------------
Windows:
  Double-click FairGame.exe.
  If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
  "Run anyway". This happens because the app isn't from a registered company.

Mac (Apple Silicon only, M1 or newer):
  Double-click FairGame. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the
  app. Click "Done", then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
  down and click "Open Anyway" next to FairGame, then double-click it again.

Linux:
  Open a terminal in this folder and run:  ./FairGame

FIRST RUN
---------
FairGame asks you a few questions in its window:
  - Which Amazon site you use (just press Enter for amazon.com).
  - The product you want. Copy the link from your browser's address bar
    while looking at the product on Amazon, and paste it in.
  - The lowest and highest price you're willing to pay.
  - Whether to do a TEST run. Say yes the first time! A test run goes all
    the way to the checkout page but does NOT buy anything.

It then asks for your Amazon email and password, and for a NEW password
that protects your Amazon login on this computer. Remember that new
password: FairGame asks for it every time it starts.

A Chrome window opens and FairGame works in it. Leave that window alone
while FairGame runs. To stop FairGame, close its black text window.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Amazon may restrict accounts that use bots. Don't run FairGame on more
  than one computer at a time with the same Amazon account.
- If Chrome offers to save your password, click "Never".
- Only turn off test mode when you're ready for FairGame to really buy.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- Forgot the FairGame password: delete config/amazon_credentials.json
  and start FairGame again.
- To change the items you're watching: start FairGame and answer "y" when it
  asks whether to change them.
- Errors are saved in the "logs" folder. The "screenshots" folder shows what
  Chrome saw at each step.
